How to use this guide

Most meetings do not fail because the wrong people are in the room. They fail because nobody says the sentence that actually moves the conversation forward, so an hour passes and three people leave with three different memories of what got decided.

This guide gives you 30 specific phrases, grouped by what they do: redirect a meeting that is drifting off course, push back on an idea without creating conflict, and close with a name attached to every decision. Each one comes with the exact line to say, when it works, when it backfires, and a real scenario showing it in context.

At the end: 8 habits that quietly kill meeting momentum, the small patterns that turn a decisive room into one that needs a follow-up meeting just to decide what the last meeting already decided.
