How to use this guide

Most people do not lose a negotiation because they asked for too much. They lose it because, in the moment, they had no language ready and defaulted to whatever kept the conversation comfortable. A recruiter asks for your number and you give one. An offer lands and you say thank you. The silence gets awkward and you fill it by agreeing.

This guide gives you 15 scripts for the specific moments that come up across a negotiation: opening the conversation, applying leverage you already have, and negotiating the parts of an offer beyond base salary that most people never touch. Each one includes the exact line to say, the mistake it prevents, and a short scenario showing it used.

At the end: 8 mistakes that quietly cost people real money, the habits that feel polite or safe in the moment and cost thousands over a career.
