How to use this guide

The higher the stakes, the less directly people speak. A board member rarely says "I don't trust this plan." They say "I'd want more data before we go all in," and the meeting moves on as if nothing was said, when in fact the entire trajectory just shifted. Missing that signal costs you the room without you ever knowing you lost it.

This guide gives you 40 phrases you'll hear in boardrooms, investor updates, and executive reviews. For each one: what it actually means, when it's the right diplomatic move to use it yourself, when it reads as evasive, a realistic example, and a tip that's usually about how to respond the moment you hear it directed at you.

At the end: 8 phrases that reveal more than the speaker intends, the tells that show up right before someone says something they've been holding back.
