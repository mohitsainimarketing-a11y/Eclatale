## Full description

Eclatale brings your AI brand assistant to LinkedIn and the entire web.

✨ WHAT IT DOES:
- Get AI post ideas while browsing LinkedIn
- Copy your LinkedIn profile sections to optimize them with AI
- Repurpose any article or content from the web with one right-click
- See your brand stats and streak without leaving LinkedIn

🔒 WHAT IT DOESN'T DO:
- Never scrapes LinkedIn or any other site
- Never accesses your LinkedIn account data or cookies
- Never reads your messages or personal information
- Uses only official LinkedIn OAuth (handled in the main Eclatale app)

Built for founders, executives, and professionals who are serious about
building their personal brand on LinkedIn.

Full data-access breakdown: eclatale.com/privacy and this repo's
extension/PRIVACY.md.
